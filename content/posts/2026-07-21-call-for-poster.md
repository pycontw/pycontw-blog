## PyCon Taiwan 2026 Call for Poster is Open!

Do you have a Python project, technical research, hands-on experience, or an exciting idea to share?
Submit your proposal to PyCon Taiwan 2026 Poster Session!

The Poster Session is an interactive format where presenters showcase their work through posters and engage in one-on-one discussions with conference attendees. Whether you're sharing an open source project, a case study, research, teaching experience, or any Python-related topic, we'd love to hear from you!

Selected posters will be exhibited at PyCon Taiwan 2026. Presenters will have the chance to connect with the community, share their ideas, receive feedback, and meet fellow Python enthusiasts! 💙

📅 Important Dates
🗓️ Poster Presentation dates: Oct. 17–18, 2026
📝 Call for Poster Begins: Jul. 21, 2026
⏰ Call for Poster Ends: Aug.  20, 2026, at 23:59:59 (AoE)

📩Announcement of Acceptance: Aug. 31, 2026
